CPAM (Ameli) - French Social Security Registration

Deadline: 6 Days Remaining
What

Register for 100% free student healthcare coverage and obtain your French provisional Social Security Number (NIR).

When

Immediately after obtaining university Certificat de Scolarité. Maximum within 30 days of arrival.

Where

LEGAL RESIDENCY DEADLINE account_balance Ministère de l'Intérieur (ANEF)

Validation of VLS-TS Visa on ANEF

timelapse 24 Days Remaining (90d rule)
What

Validate your long-stay visa to give it legal value as a Residence Permit (Titre de séjour) and pay the €50 student tax timbre.

When

Strictly within 90 days after landing in France. Past this limit, residency is technically irregular.

Where

administration-etrangers-en-france.interieur.gouv.fr open_in_new

Prerequisites to Have Ready Before Validation
check Visa Number (VLS-TS sticker) check Official Date of Entry (Passport Stamp) check Fixed Nantes Residence Address check Carte Bancaire (CB) for €50 Tax Stamp
lightbulb
CampusLink Advisory

Do not wait for the final week. You will receive an immediate PDF download titled Confirmation de la validation de l'enregistrement de votre visa. This specific PDF is strictly requested by both CAF for housing benefits and HR for student employment contracts!

CAF - Demande d'Aide Personnalisée au Logement (APL)

Regular Priority
What

Government rent subsidy paid each month directly to your student bank account or deducted directly from CROUS rent.

When

Immediately after moving in and signing your lease. CAF does not pay retroactively for past months.

Where
