## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sophistication, clarity, and social ethos of modern European civic tech and premium SaaS. Built for international students navigating their arrival and integration in France, the aesthetic blends the precision of contemporary French institutional tech with the warmth, approachability, and dynamism expected by digital-native students.

### Brand Persona & Emotional Architecture
- **Empathetic & Guiding:** Administrative procedures (CAF, Ameli, Titre de Séjour) are naturally stressful. The interface acts as a calm, trustworthy companion that breaks bureaucratic complexity into serene, deterministic steps.
- **Civic & Sustainable:** Deep emerald and forest accents reflect European eco-consciousness, student solidarity, and sustainable living initiatives (second-hand markets, low-emission travel, social engagement).
- **Precision-Driven:** Rooted in clean modernist editorial balance, featuring generous breathing room, intentional hierarchy, and distinct visual categorizations that make crucial dates and requirements unmissable.

### Visual Style
A tailored hybrid of **Refined Corporate / Modern European SaaS** and **Soft Tactile Surfaces**:
- Ultra-clean canvas surfaces layered over pale slate backdrops.
- Precision lines with featherweight borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- Deep atmospheric multi-layered diffuse shadows that provide distinct z-axis hierarchy without heavy skeuomorphic clutter.
- Pill-shaped status containers with low-saturation chromatic fills to denote urgency, institutional credibility, or environmental impact.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for cognitive clarity, systematic triaging of administrative priorities, and distinct institutional vs. ecological contexts.

### Primary Spectrum (French Tech Blue)
- **Primary Base (`#2563EB`)**: Anchor for administrative flows, procedural tasks, primary CTA buttons, and state indicators.
- **Primary Hover/Active (`#1D4ED8`)**: Used for focused interactions and high-contrast link states.
- **Primary Subdued (`#EFF6FF`)**: Background tint for administrative badges, step indicators, and informational banners.

### Secondary Spectrum (Eco & Solidarity Emerald)
- **Secondary Base (`#059669`)**: Sustainability indices, student impact scores, circular economy listings, and student solidarity initiatives.
- **Secondary Light (`#10B981`)**: Positive feedback states, checklist completion rings, and verified indicators.
- **Secondary Dark (`#047857`)**: Legible text on tinted backgrounds and high-contrast data visualizations.
- **Secondary Subdued (`#ECFDF5`)**: Pill background for eco-features, verified student badges, and positive alerts.

### Semantic & Urgency Accents
- **Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`) / Alert Orange (`#EA580C`)**: Urgent deadlines, expiring visa alerts, housing deposit cutoffs, and impending steps.
- **Orange Subdued (`#FFF7ED`)**: Tint for urgency containers and expiring status chips.
- **Critical Crimson (`#DC2626`)**: Critical administrative blocks, missing legal paperwork, and medical/blood donation drives.
- **Crimson Subdued (`#FEF2F2`)**: Urgent callout backgrounds.

### Neutrals & Structural Foundations
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)**: Page backdrop delivering crisp contrast against white modules.
- **Surface Secondary (`#F1F5F9`)**: Sidebar rails, disabled fields, nested card tracks, and tab bars.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary elevated cards, dialogs, and popovers.
- **Structural Stroke (`#E2E8F0`)**: Hairline borders defining surfaces and interactive regions.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Headings, dominant data values, and high-emphasis labels.
- **Text Secondary (`#475569`)**: Supporting body prose, meta information, and field descriptors.
- **Text Muted (`#94A3B8`)**: Placeholders, disabled states, and auxiliary timestamps.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the sole typographic voice across headlines, body copy, and UI metadata. Its geometric construction, subtle humanist curves, and tall x-height deliver unmatched readability across both multi-lingual official forms and conversational guides.

### Editorial Hierarchy Rules
- **Headlines (`display`, `headline-lg`, `headline-md`)**: Rendered in heavier weights (700-800) with slight negative tracking to produce cohesive, editorial anchors on onboarding screens, status overviews, and module headers.
- **Body (`body-lg`, `body-md`)**: Scaled at comfortable line heights (1.55-1.6) with pure slate colors (`#0F172A` and `#475569`) to reduce cognitive fatigue during dense legal or administrative reading.
- **Badges & Labels (`label-sm`)**: Uppercase styling is strictly reserved for short institutional tokens (e.g., `CAF`, `AMELI`, `OFFICIAL`). All user-facing tags default to capitalized sentence-case with bold weight (700) for rapid scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances information density with generous whitespace to prevent administrative overload. 

### Grid Philosophy
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: A 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum container width of `1280px`. Typical compositions feature an asymmetric split: an 8-column main operational cockpit alongside a 4-column companion rail for urgent timelines, assistant advice callouts, and progress meters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: An 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Complementary rails collapse into stacked summary sheets or accessible bottom tabs.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: A 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. All primary task modules scale to full canvas width, utilizing horizontal snap carousels for multi-step progress indicators.

### Component Spacing Cadence
- **`space-xs` (4px)**: Micro-gaps between badge dot-indicators and labels.
- **`space-sm` (8px)**: Distance between input labels, helper text, and icon-to-text button pairings.
- **`space-md` (16px)**: Standard interior padding for compact cards, list cells, search fields, and status bars.
- **`space-lg` (24px)**: Primary interior padding for all major elevated cards, interactive mini-maps, and assistant callouts.
- **`space-xl` (32px)**: Section groupings within cards and margins separating unrelated administrative flows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp borders and soft, diffused atmospheric shadows, avoiding muddy blurs.

### Surface Tiers & Depth
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC`. Background layer for page architecture.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a clean 1px stroke of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Used for passive dashboards and static content blocks.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Mini-Maps)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` stroke. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Used for interactive checklist cards, filter dropdowns, and search bars.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs, Overlays & Sticky Bars)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Tinted Context Banners)**: Assistant advice callouts use no drop shadow; instead, they employ a continuous 1px border stroke with a subtle 50% opacity color fill (e.g., `#EFF6FF` for administrative or `#ECFDF5` for eco-initiatives).

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses friendly, modern, rounded geometry to soften governmental workflows while retaining SaaS rigor.

### Rounding Hierarchy
- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Styled with `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / 16px to `1.25rem` / 20px) to form self-contained, welcoming content capsules.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs & Mini-Maps**: Structured with standard roundedness (`0.5rem` / 8px to `0.75rem` / 12px) for sharp, tactile affordance.
- **Pill Badges, Chips & Progress Bars**: Fully rounded (`9999px`) to create distinction between actionable cards and glanceable status data.
- **Strokes & Insets**: Interactive cards and input fields maintain a unified 1px solid stroke (`#E2E8F0`), transitioning to a 2px offset focus ring (`#2563EB`) on direct interaction.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (French Tech Blue)**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, white typography, `0.75rem` rounded corners, padding `0.75rem 1.25rem`. Hover shifts to `#1D4ED8`. Shadow: subtle blue halo `0 4px 12px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary Action (Eco / Solidarité)**: Solid `#059669` fill, white typography. Used for community contributions, eco-actions, and sustainable swap markets.
- **Outline / Ghost**: `#FFFFFF` background, `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9` background.

### 2. Setup Progress Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-2xl` radius, `space-lg` padding, and `#E2E8F0` border.
- Features a two-part header: a category tag alongside a percentage tracker.
- Includes a 6px continuous progress track in `#F1F5F9` with a rounded, multi-stop emerald-to-blue active fill.
- Displays nested checklists where completed steps trigger a strike-through with an emerald `#059669` checkmark badge.

### 3. Urgency Badges & Source Tags
- **Official Source Tag**: Pill badge with `#EFF6FF` background, `#1D4ED8` text, and a blue check icon indicator.
- **Community Source Tag**: Pill badge with `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, and an outline avatar/people icon.
- **Urgent Deadline Badge**: Pill badge with `#FFF7ED` background, `#EA580C` text, and a pulsing dot indicator (`#F59E0B`).
- **Critical Action Badge**: Pill badge with `#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, reserved for legal deadlines, visa renewals, or blood donation appeals.

### 4. Interactive Mini-Maps
- Embedded cards with `rounded-2xl` clipping and a continuous `#E2E8F0` border.
- Map canvas uses a desaturated, light European cartography skin with custom primary blue and emerald map markers.
- Features an overlay floating glass control bar with quick-filter pills: "CPAM Offices", "CROUS Dining", "Eco-Markets", and "Prefecture".

### 5. Assistant Advice Callouts
- Non-modal, full-width advisory cards built with a left vertical accent bar (3px solid `#2563EB` or `#059669`).
- Tinted background matching the advisory context (`#EFF6FF` for administrative tips, `#ECFDF5` for student budget/eco advice).
- Top row highlights a dedicated avatar or mascot micro-badge paired with a short "Conseil de CampusLink" heading in `label-md` weight.

### 6. Form Inputs & Search Filters
- Inputs feature pure white surfaces, `0.625rem` border radius, `#E2E8F0` hairline borders, and `space-md` inner padding.
- Focused state applies a dual-ring: 1px border `#2563EB` plus an external `3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` glow.
- Search filters use rounded pill shapes with horizontal scrolling containers, allowing rapid multi-select filtering for housing types, language requirements, or campus zones.
